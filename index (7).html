      About us

      About PRACY

      PRACY is a B2B marketplace where licensed medicine retailers and licensed wholesalers do business in one place. A pharmacy can find a supplier, compare the best offers and pay. A wholesaler can reach new customers and manage orders and staff easily.

      Trust comes first in medicine. Every wholesaler submits a TIN certificate, a BRELA certificate of incorporation and the MEMART, and any other documents required by regulators. Every retailer submits a TIN number and a NIDA number. Our team reviews them before anyone can buy or sell.

      Our story

      Africa doesn’t just need more medicine. It needs a better way to move medicine.

      Across Africa, the challenge of accessing medicines is not simply about how much medicine is available. It is also about how efficiently medicines move from manufacturers to wholesalers, from wholesalers to retailers, and ultimately to the people who need them.

      For too long, fragmented supply chains, disconnected markets, limited price transparency, and inefficient procurement processes have made pharmaceutical trade more complicated than it should be.

      We believe Africa deserves better.

      At PRACY, we believe that improving access to medicine starts with transforming the way the pharmaceutical industry connects, trades, and operates.

      That is why we are building PRACY, a digital marketplace that brings pharmaceutical manufacturers, wholesalers and retailers closer together through one connected ecosystem.

      Retailers deserve better visibility into available products, suppliers and prices. Wholesalers and manufacturers deserve a more accessible way to reach the businesses that need their products.

      Through PRACY, we are working to create a more connected marketplace where pharmaceutical businesses can discover opportunities, make informed decisions and trade with greater confidence.

        Quick answers

        How do I know a wholesaler is genuine?

        Every wholesaler uploads a TIN certificate, a BRELA certificate of incorporation and the MEMART. Only wholesalers approved by the admin appear in the marketplace.

        How do I pay?

        Press Buy on the medicines you want, open Cart and press Buy now. You see each wholesaler's bank or mobile money account, send the money, and enter your payment reference. The wholesaler confirms when it arrives.

        What if my order is wrong or late?

        Open Help, pick the order and describe the problem. The admin reviews it, replies to both sides, and can refund the order or pause an account.

        Can my staff use PRACY?

        Yes. Wholesalers add order clerks and delivery staff in the Team tab. Staff see orders only, not stock, payouts or sales.

        When can I start trading?

        You can log in right away, but you can buy or sell only after the admin approves your documents.
